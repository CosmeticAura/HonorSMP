# Changelog

## 1.6.0
- Stronger honorable ranks (Honorable / Noble / Paragon): +2/4/6 max health (new `healthBonusHp`), villager trades 10/20/30% cheaper (was 5/10/15), beneficial potions last +20/35/50% longer (was 15/30/50), hunger drains 15/25/35% slower (was 10/15/20). Noble and up keep XP on death, Paragon also keeps the hotbar (slots 0 to 8) and offhand and has a gold name in chat. Kept items do not also drop. `/honor` shows the new perks. Dishonored perks are unchanged.
- Civ upgrades: `/civ upgrades` opens a chest menu (leader and officers buy, members can look). Resistance 5/10% less damage, Strength +0.5/+1 attack damage, Speed +5/10%, Haste I/II, Fortitude +2/+4 max health. Level 1 costs 32 emeralds, level 2 costs 64 emeralds and 8 diamonds, taken from the buyer's inventory. They apply to all online members and stack with rank perks. Disbanding the civ loses them with no refund.
- The civ tax is back, now only for upgrades: 4 emeralds per owned upgrade level per member per week. `/civ tax` shows what is owed and when, `/civ tax pay` (leader or officer) pays into the judges' treasury. Warning on login and 24 hours before. If it is late all the civ's upgrades switch off and `[Unpaid]` shows in `/civ info` and `/civ list`; nobody loses honor and paying turns them back on at once.
- Donor tiers by lifetime honor earned from donating: Supporter (200) gets a `[Supporter]` tag and 20% slower hunger (rank and donor hunger perks together capped at 50%), Patron (750) gets permanent Luck, Benefactor (2000) gets Regeneration I for 30 seconds on every respawn and a gold `[Benefactor]` tag. Every Monday 00:00 server time the top donor of the past 7 days gets Hero of the Village I for 7 days and a server-wide message. `/honor` and the `/donate` info item show your total, tier and the distance to the next. Judges get no donor perks.
- Judges now get exactly the Paragon perks (health, discounts, potion and hunger bonuses, keep XP and hotbar, gold name, Bounty Compass). No honor changes, no donor perks, still `[Judge]`.
- `/donate` takes ores, ingots and ore blocks. Common materials (new cap type `common_materials`, 30 honor a day): coal 1, coal block 9, raw copper / copper ingot 1, copper block 9, raw iron / iron ingot 5, iron block 45, raw gold / gold ingot 5, gold block 45, redstone 1, redstone block 9, lapis 2, lapis block 18, nether quartz 1, quartz block 4, amethyst shard 1. Rare materials (`rare_materials`, 40 honor a day): emerald 20, emerald block 180, diamond 50, diamond block 450, netherite scrap 150, netherite ingot 600, netherite block 5400. Values are points (10 points = 1 honor). Silk-touched ore blocks count as what they drop. Same rules as food: plain items only, the overall daily cap still applies, items past a cap are still shared but earn no honor.
- New config keys: `healthBonusHp`, `keepXpMinRank`, `keepHotbarMinRank`, `goldNameMinRank`, `perksVersion`, `civUpgradesEnabled`, `upgradeEmeraldCost`, `upgradeDiamondCost`, `upgradeResistancePercent`, `upgradeStrengthBonus`, `upgradeSpeedPercent`, `upgradeHasteLevel`, `upgradeFortitudeHp`, `taxPerLevelPerMemberPerWeek`, `taxIntervalDays`, `taxWarningHours`, `donorSupporterMin`, `donorPatronMin`, `donorBenefactorMin`, `donorHungerSlowPercent`, `donorHungerCapPercent`, `donorLuckBonus`, `donorRespawnRegenSeconds`, `topDonorEnabled`, `topDonorHeroDays`, `donateCapTypes`, `donateMaterialsVersion`.
- Admin notes: the rank perk values changed. On first load the old honorable perk arrays (`potionBonusPercent` 15/30/50, `tradeDiscountPercent` 5/10/15, `hungerSlowPercent` 10/15/20) are replaced by the new ones only if they are still exactly the old defaults; edited values are kept (`perksVersion` becomes 1). The new donate materials are added to `donateExtraItems` and `donateCapTypes` only where missing, and the new caps to `donateItemDailyCaps`. `donatedHonorTotal` is filled in once from the "Donated items" / "Donated food" log entries. Civs and the rest of `data.json` load unchanged; new civ upgrade fields start empty.
- Known limit: XP and items kept on death are held in memory until respawn. If the server restarts while a player is on the death screen they are lost.

## 1.5.0
- Civ taxes are gone. `/civ tax`, `/civ tax pay`, the weekly emerald tax, the late penalty and the `[Unpaid]` tag are removed, and so are the config keys `taxPerMember`, `taxIntervalDays`, `taxWarnHours` and `taxLatePenaltyHonor`. Tax fields in old `data.json` are dropped on load. The Treasury stays (paid fines, `/honor treasury`).
- Civ chat: `/cc` toggles it. While on, your chat goes only to online members of your civ. Not saved across restarts.
- No friendly fire: members of the same civ cannot hurt each other (melee, arrows, tridents, projectiles, harmful potion effects) or each other's tamed pets.
- New `/civ top`: top 10 civs by the total honor of their members (judges not counted).
- New `/civ chest`: a shared 54 slot chest for each civ. Leader, officers, members and guards can use it, one member at a time. Saved when it closes (also on disconnect, death, kick, leave and server stop) and every 30 seconds while open. If the civ is disbanded, the contents go to the leader's `/donation` box. `/honor reset all` clears it.
- Raid sieges: a civ with a home (at least 2 members whose overworld bed respawn points are within 100 blocks of each other) gets a random siege every 2 to 4 real days, when at least 2 members are online within 100 blocks of home. 60 seconds warning (title and chat "A raid is coming to <civ> in 60 seconds!", server-wide "Pillagers are marching on <civ>!"). 3 waves: pillagers and vindicators, then a witch and an evoker, then a ravager and a "Siege Captain". A boss bar shows progress and the next wave comes 20 seconds after the last mob dies. The civ loses if nobody is within 150 blocks of home for 2 minutes or 15 minutes pass. A win gives +10 honor to every member who damaged siege mobs (judges excepted), a loot chest at home and "<civ> defended their home!" to the server.
- Judges: `/honor siege <civ>` starts a siege now (5 second warning, needs a home and 2 members near it), `/honor siege stop <civ>` ends one (counts as a loss).
- New config keys for sieges: `siegesEnabled`, `siegeMinDays`, `siegeMaxDays`, `siegeHomeRadius`, `siegeMinMembersOnline`, `siegeWarnSeconds`, `siegeSpawnMinDistance`, `siegeSpawnMaxDistance`, `siegeMobsPerMember`, `siegeMaxMobsPerWave`, `siegeWaveDelaySeconds`, `siegeAbandonRadius`, `siegeAbandonSeconds`, `siegeMaxMinutes`, `siegeRewardHonor`, `siegeCaptainHealth`, `siegeLoot`.
- Donations: the per-item leftover pot is replaced by a rotation. What does not split evenly goes to different players each time, so everyone gets the same over time. Players who are online are told "You got ... from <donor>'s donation. Use /donation to claim it." `/donation` with nothing waiting says "Nothing to claim right now...".
- Donation rewards are bigger: `honorPerHungerPoints` is now 10 (a stack of bread = 32 honor) and `donateDailyCap` is now 100. New per-type daily honor caps in `donateItemDailyCaps`: food combined 60, golden apple 20, enchanted golden apple 30, totem of undying 32, ender pearl 16. Items past a cap are still accepted and shared, they just stop earning honor. The info item in the `/donate` chest lists each type's remaining honor and what the chest would give. Leftover points still carry over to the next donation, now kept per item type.
- Migration: on first load the config gets `donateRewardsVersion` 1. `honorPerHungerPoints` 20 becomes 10 and `donateDailyCap` 25 becomes 100, only if they still have the old value (changed values are kept). Missing `donateItemDailyCaps` entries are added. Old `donatePots` are handed out into the players' `donateBoxes` at startup (the leftover pots are emptied). Civ tax fields are dropped, and new fields (`chest`, `nextSiegeAt`, `respawnPoints`) start empty.

## 1.4.0
- Fixed `/donate` with extra words (`/donate food`): it now just opens the chest. Command errors now say what to do instead of a bare failure. New automated test runs the player commands as a non-op.
- Donations are shared: Confirm no longer goes to the treasury. The items are split equally between every other player who has ever joined and land in their `/donation` claim box (chest UI, take only, paged, saved across restarts, dupe protected). What does not split evenly stays in a per-item leftover pot for the next donation of that item. With nobody else to share with, nothing is taken. Players with items waiting are told on login, and everyone online gets a broadcast. Food donated before 1.4.0 stays in the treasury.
- New donatable items: golden apple (40 points), enchanted golden apple (300), totem of undying (160), ender pearl (5). Same rate of 1 honor per 20 points and the same 25 a day cap. Items with a custom name, enchantments or other changed components are refused. Judges can donate but earn no honor. Honor shows as "Donated items" in `/honor log`.
- Automatic honor events, all shown with a reason in `/honor log`, judges exempt, daily caps reset at midnight server time: cure a zombie villager +10 (max 30 a day), harvest mature wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot or nether wart by hand +1 per 64 (max 10 a day, not in creative or spectator), breed animals +1 per 5 (max 5 a day), trade with villagers +1 per 10 (max 5 a day), win a raid +15, help kill the Wither, Ender Dragon or Warden +15 (once per boss type per day), kill a villager or wandering trader -15, kill another player's tamed animal -20.
- Known limit: boss fight damage is tracked in memory, so a server restart mid-fight forfeits that fight's rewards.
- Config: new keys `donateExtraItems`, `zombieCureHonor`, `zombieCureDailyCap`, `harvestPerCrops`, `harvestHonor`, `harvestDailyCap`, `breedPerBreeds`, `breedHonor`, `breedDailyCap`, `tradePerTrades`, `tradeHonor`, `tradeDailyCap`, `raidWinHonor`, `bossKillHonor`, `villagerKillPenalty`, `petKillPenalty`. Existing configs keep their values and get the defaults for new keys.
- Migration: `data.json` gets `donateBoxes`, `donatePots` and per-player counters for the automatic events. Old data loads unchanged. `/honor reset all` clears the new donation data too.

## 1.3.0
- New `/donate` for players (judges cannot use it): opens a 27 slot chest where only food goes in (anything edible plus cake). The bottom row has Back, Close, an info item (honor the contents are worth and honor still earnable today) and a green wool Confirm.
- 1 honor per 20 hunger points donated, rounded down (a stack of bread is 64 x 5 = 320 hunger = 16 honor). Leftover hunger points carry over to the next donation. Daily cap of 25 honor from donating, reset at midnight server time; once reached the chest says so and refuses more food.
- Rotten flesh, spider eyes, poisonous potatoes, pufferfish and suspicious stew are worth 0.
- Confirm takes the food, gives honor logged as "Donated food" and puts the food in the judges' treasury. Closing without confirming gives everything back; disconnect, death and server stop also return the items.
- New config keys `honorPerHungerPoints` (20), `donateDailyCap` (25), `donateCakeHunger` (14, cake has no food component) and `donateZeroFoods` (the five foods above).
- The playtime bonus is off by default: `onlineBonusHonor` now defaults to 0 (`onlineBonusHours` stays 3). With 0 it gives no honor and writes no log entry. Existing configs that set a value keep it. Honor now comes from PvP, judge awards and donations.

## 1.2.1
- Fixed the netherite smithing upgrade losing the upgrade template: the diamond item, the template and the ingot all come back. The found-netherite conversion path never takes a template.
- New `/honor reset all` (ops only, run twice within 10 s, backs up `data.json` first). Wipes honor, civs, taxes, bounties and bounty boxes, cases, appeals, fines, treasury, logs and anti-farm data. Judges stay. Items in bounty boxes, the treasury and open chest screens are deleted. Announced in chat.
- Judges can rule on any case, including ones they are part of.
- Cases can be renamed: Rename button (name tag) in the case menu or `/honor case <id> rename <name>`. The name shows in `/honor cases`, the case book and the Discord posts.
- Back button on every chest page opened from another page, keeping the list page. Top level lists have a Close button. Long lists are paged.
- A kill between players on the same IP no longer pays out a player-set bounty (it stays on the target) and is flagged as a case.

## 1.2.0
- Honor sidebar: each player sees their own tier and honor on the right of the screen (per-player, server-side).
- Judge tier: the highest honorable rank, gold tag above Paragon. No automatic honor changes for judges and `/honor` shows `Judge`. Killing a judge is an unprovoked Paragon kill for honor; a dishonored killer gets the new `judgeLoot` pool plus a server-wide announcement.
- Kills are decided automatically when the mod can tell who started the fight. Only unclear cases go to a judge. New `/appeal <reason>` (or `<id> <reason>`) for your latest or a chosen honor change, one open appeal at a time. Judges get a written `Case #N` book for each case (on next login if offline).
- Chest GUIs: `/honor cases` with a case menu (Confirm, Cancel, Set amount sign, Fine, Give book, Back), own cases greyed out, and `/bounty list` with heads and a read-only loot view. Items are icons only, no taking or duping.
- Fines: `/fine <player>`, `/fine`, `/fine pay`, `/fine list`, `/fine cancel <id>`. Paid items go to the treasury. Unpaid fines cost honor every `fineIntervalHours` (2.5), `fineHonorPenalty` (10), also offline. New config keys `fineIntervalHours` and `fineHonorPenalty`.
- Bounty Compass: no target shows "No wanted players nearby" and spins, and it stops working below Noble with "You must be Noble or higher to use this".
- `/honor top` shows only currently honorable players on top and currently dishonored players on the bottom.
- `/civ promote` says "Already an officer", `/civ guard` says what changed, `/civ invite` says the player is already a member.
- Fixed `/bounty claim` giving nothing after a bounty kill; the claim box now survives restarts.
- Enchant caps for everyone (`enchantCaps`): Sharpness 4, Protection 3, Density 4, Breach 3. Only the capped enchant is lowered.
- No netherite swords, axes or armor: smithing upgrades are cancelled and refunded, existing pieces convert to diamond with the same enchants, durability and name, plus one netherite ingot. Netherite pickaxes, shovels and hoes are allowed.
- Elytra combat lock: no opening an elytra for 30 seconds after player damage (`combatElytraSeconds`).

## 1.1.0
- Removed land claiming: no `/civ claim` or `/civ unclaim`, claim limits or storage. Removed the honor events for blocks, containers and villagers or golems in claims, and the sleep in claim rule. Dishonored downsides are now shorter potions and pricier villagers. Killing a dishonored player gives +35 to guards and +25 to everyone else, anywhere. Old claim data is dropped on load.
- `/civ create` is free. Civs pay a weekly tax to the judges (default 8 emeralds per member, every 7 days). `/civ tax` and `/civ tax pay` (leader or officer, early payment allowed). Late civs lose 10 honor per member per day and show [Unpaid]. Warnings on login and 24 hours before due. New config: `taxPerMember`, `taxIntervalDays`, `taxLatePenaltyHonor`, `taxWarnHours`.
- Paid tax goes to the Treasury, opened by judges with `/honor treasury`.
- Ops are no longer judges. Only players added with `/honor judge add` can use `cases`, `case`, `give`, `log` and the treasury. Ops keep admin commands. Judges cannot rule on cases they are part of.
- A dishonored player killing an honorable player gets a loot pool instead of emeralds (2/3/4 rolls for Honorable/Noble/Paragon victims), configurable with `bountyLoot`. Overflow drops at the killer's feet.
- Bounties use chest UIs. `/bounty set <player>` takes any items, stacks across players, dishonored targets only. Payouts go to a bounty box opened with `/bounty claim`. `/bounty list` shows item and stack counts. Escrow is saved on close, disconnect, death and server stop. Season reset keeps bounties. 1.0.0 emerald bounties become emerald stacks.
