=== how-1
**Honor**

**Goes up when you:**
> Kill a player with negative honor: +25
> Donate food, ores and gems with `/donate`: up to +100 a day
> Help your civ win a siege: +10
> Do something good and show the Judge proof

**Goes down when you:**
> Kill someone who didn't hit you first: -30
> Kill someone with no weapon and no armor: -60
> Don't pay a fine

Self defense is free: if they hit you first, you lose nothing.

=== how-2
**Ranks**

> Paragon: 800+
> Noble: 400+
> Honorable: 100+
> Neutral: around 0
> Outlaw: -100 or less
> Marauder: -400 or less
> Warlord: -800 or less

**High honor:** more hearts, longer potions, cheaper villagers, less hunger. Noble keeps XP when you die. Paragon keeps XP and hotbar. Judges get the Paragon perks.
**Low honor:** hidden from tab, extra damage and speed, but anyone can put a bounty on you.

=== how-3
**Good to know**

**Bounties:** `/bounty set <player>` to put items on an outlaw. Kill them, then `/bounty claim`.
**Civs (teams):** `/civ create <name>` is free. Use `/cc` for team chat and `/civ chest` for a shared chest (needs 3 members in your civ, starts with 1 row, the leader buys more rows in /civ upgrades, locked for 5 minutes after another player hurts you). Teammates can't hurt each other.
**Civ upgrades:** `/civ upgrades` buys team boosts, paid in iron, gunpowder, redstone, lapis and gold (no emeralds). Each one has its own small daily upkeep, paid in the same menu. Late upkeep turns only that upgrade off until it's paid, and the Pause button stops one costing.
**Donor ranks:** donate a lot and you get a tag, then Luck, then healing when you respawn. The top donor each week gets Hero of the Village.
**Sieges:** every few days pillagers attack a team's home. Defend it for +10 honor and a loot chest.
**Unfair call?** `/appeal <reason>`

**Server changes**
> Max Sharpness 4, Protection 3, Density 4, Breach 3
> No netherite swords, axes or armor (netherite tools are fine)
> Elytra won't open for 30 seconds after a player hits you
> Golden apple: 4 gold ingots around 1 apple (plus shape)
> Shulker box: 8 obsidian around 1 chest

=== commands
**Commands**

`/honor` your honor
`/honor top` leaderboard
`/donate` give food and ores for honor
`/bounty set <player>` put a bounty on an outlaw
`/bounty claim` get bounty loot
`/bounty list` see who's wanted
`/civ create <name>` start a team
`/civ invite <player>` invite someone
`/civ join <name>` join a team
`/cc` team chat on or off
`/civ chest` shared team chest (needs 3 members)
`/civ top` best teams
`/civ upgrades` team upgrades: buy, pay and pause, all in one menu
`/fine pay` pay a fine
`/appeal <reason>` think a call was unfair?
