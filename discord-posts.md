## start-here

**Welcome to Honor SMP**

This is a near vanilla survival server with one twist: everything you do earns or costs you honor.

Build, trade and protect people and you'll rise toward Paragon. Raid, grief and kill whoever you want and you'll sink toward Warlord. Both are real ways to play. Honorable players get an easier life. Dishonored players get stealth, bounties and a combat edge.

**Join:** [SERVER IP], Java Edition 1.21.11, no mods needed on your end.

Before you play:
1. Read #rules
2. Read #how-it-works so you know what your choices cost
3. Keep #commands open the first time you log in

Got a problem with a player, go to #report-a-player. Something broken, #report-a-bug.

## rules

**Server rules**

Honor handles most of what you do in game. These are the few things that get you banned instead.

1. No hacked clients, x-ray, duping or exploits. If you find a bug, report it, don't use it.
2. No alts used to farm honor or bounties. The mod checks for this.
3. No slurs, real threats or harassment in chat or voice. Trash talk is fine, going after someone personally is not.
4. No lag machines or anything built to crash the server.
5. Don't argue with a judge's ruling in public chat. If you think a ruling was wrong, post in #report-a-player with your side.
6. Alts on the same IP never pay out bounties. Those kills are flagged and reviewed.

Everything else, including raiding, griefing and killing people, is allowed. It just costs honor.

## how-it-works

**Honor**

Everyone starts at 0. Your score decides your side.

Honorable side
> Paragon: 800 and up
> Noble: 400 to 799
> Honorable: 100 to 399

Judge: the highest rank, above Paragon. Judges have no score and honor rules do not apply to them.

> Neutral: -99 to 99

Dishonored side
> Outlaw: -100 to -399
> Marauder: -400 to -799
> Warlord: -800 and below

**What the honorable get**
Longer potion effects (up to +50%), cheaper villager trades, slower hunger. Nobles and up can get a Bounty Compass that tracks wanted outlaws. Your tier and honor show on the right side of your screen at all times.

**What the dishonored get**
Hidden from the tab list, name tag hidden while sneaking, extra attack damage and speed, and a loot pool for every honorable player they kill (better loot for higher tiers). The catch: shorter potions and pricier villagers. Kill the judge and you get a huge loot pool and the whole server hears about it.

**How honor changes**

The mod handles the obvious stuff:
> Killing a dishonored player: +25 (+35 if you're a guard)
> Killing in self defense: no change
> Killing someone unarmed with no armor: -60
> Donating with /donate: 1 honor per 10 points (a stack of bread is 32), up to 100 a day. Food, golden apples, totems, ender pearls, common ores and rare gems also have their own daily limits. Emeralds don't count. Ores, ingots and silk-touched ore blocks count too (a diamond is 5 honor).
> Curing a zombie villager: +10 (30 a day max)
> Harvesting 64 grown crops by hand: +1 (10 a day max)
> Breeding 5 animals: +1 (5 a day max). Trading 10 times with villagers: +1 (5 a day max)
> Winning a raid: +15
> Helping kill the Wither, Ender Dragon or Warden: +15 (once per boss a day)
> Killing a villager or wandering trader: -15
> Killing another player's tamed pet: -20
> Defending your civ from a siege: +10

The mod decides most kills by itself by checking who hit first, gear and tiers. Only the kills it can't call go to a judge. They get a case book with who hit first and what everyone was wearing, then confirm or change the call. Think a call was wrong? Use /appeal and your reason (your latest change, or /appeal and a case number for an older one). You get one open appeal at a time and each change can be appealed once. Judges are players the admins picked, not ops. Judges can rule on any case, even their own, so expect the other judges to check anything that looks off. They can also award honor for good deeds and big builds, and fine players. A fine is a set of items you pay with /fine pay, and every 2.5 hours it stays unpaid costs you 10 honor. Players can also turn spare food, golden apples, totems and ender pearls into honor with /donate. Donated items are shared equally with every other player on the server and wait in their /donation box. What does not split evenly rotates between players, so everyone gets the same over time, and you get a message when you receive something. Past a daily limit you can still donate, you just earn no honor. Judges earn no honor from donating or from the events above.

**Civilizations**

Start one with /civ create. It's free, and there's no land to claim.

Your civ has its own chat (/cc turns it on and off), a shared chest (/civ chest, one person at a time, needs a civ of at least 3 members; it starts with 1 row and the leader or officers buy rows 2 to 6 one by one in /civ upgrades), and a leaderboard (/civ top). You can't open the chest for 5 minutes after another player hurt you, and not while your civ is under siege. Members can't hurt each other, not even with arrows, potions or pets.

**Sieges**

If at least 2 members of your civ have their bed (respawn point) within 100 blocks of each other, that is your civ's home. Every 2 to 4 days, when 2 or more members are online near home, a raid comes. You get 60 seconds of warning ("A raid is coming to your civ in 60 seconds!") and the whole server hears about it. Three waves of pillagers and vindicators, then witches and evokers, then a ravager and the Siege Captain. A boss bar shows how it's going. Hold the home: everyone who fought gets +10 honor and a loot chest appears at home. Leave (nobody within 150 blocks for 2 minutes) or take longer than 15 minutes and you lose.

**Civ upgrades**

`/civ upgrades` opens a menu where your leader or an officer can buy five upgrades for the whole civ: Resistance (5% then 10% less damage), Strength (+0.5 then +1 attack damage), Speed (+5% then +10%), Haste (I then II) and Fortitude (+2 then +4 max health). Each upgrade runs on its own item: Resistance on iron ingots, Strength on gunpowder, Speed on redstone, Haste on lapis lazuli and Fortitude on gold ingots. Buying level 1 costs 4 days of that upgrade's upkeep, level 2 costs 4 days of its bigger upkeep plus 8 diamonds. Each upgrade has its own upkeep, paid every day (for example 16 iron ingots a day for Resistance 1, 32 for Resistance 2) by the leader or an officer with /civ upkeep pay, and it goes to the judges' treasury. You can pay each upgrade up to 3 days ahead. A civ with no upgrades pays nothing. If one upgrade's upkeep is late, only that upgrade switches off and your civ shows [Unpaid] until it's paid. Don't want an upgrade for a while? /civ upkeep pause <upgrade> turns it off and stops the cost, and paying it turns it back on. Nobody loses honor. No emeralds anywhere.

**Donor perks**

Honor you earn from donating adds up for good. At 200 you become a Supporter (a tag in chat and 20% slower hunger), at 750 a Patron (permanent Luck) and at 2,000 a Benefactor (gold tag and Regeneration for 30 seconds every time you respawn). Every Monday at midnight server time, whoever earned the most donation honor in the last 7 days gets Hero of the Village for a week, and the server is told. /honor shows your total and the next tier.

**Rank perks (1.6.0)**

Higher ranks are stronger now. Honorable, Noble and Paragon get +1, +2 and +3 hearts, 10, 20 and 30% cheaper villager trades, 20, 35 and 50% longer good potions and 15, 25 and 35% slower hunger. Noble and up keep their XP when they die. Paragon also keeps the hotbar and offhand and has a gold name. Judges get the same perks as Paragon.

Leaders can name guards. Guards are the PvP players of an honorable civ: they hunt outlaws and get more honor for it.

**Bounties**

Anyone can put items on a dishonored player's head with /bounty set. A kill between players on the same IP never pays a bounty. A chest opens, drop in what you want to offer and close it. Close it empty to cancel. Bounties from different players stack, and you can't target yourself or your own civ. Whoever kills them gets everything in their bounty box, opened with /bounty claim. /bounty list shows who is wanted.

**Limits for everyone**
Sharpness is capped at 4, Protection at 3, Density at 4 and Breach at 3. Netherite swords, axes and armor don't exist here: they turn into diamond with the same enchants, and you get a netherite ingot. Netherite pickaxes, shovels and hoes are fine. After you take damage from a player, your elytra won't open for 30 seconds.

## commands

**Everyone**
`/honor` your score, tier, perks and recent changes
`/honor top` top 10 and bottom 10
`/honor tracker` get a Bounty Compass (Noble and up)
`/bounty set <player>` put items on a dishonored player (chest opens)
`/bounty claim` collect bounty items you've earned
`/bounty list` active bounties (chest opens)
`/appeal <reason>` appeal your latest honor change
`/fine` see your open fines
`/fine pay` pay a fine (chest opens)
`/donate` donate items for honor, shared with everyone else (chest opens)
`/donation` collect items others donated to you (chest opens)

**Civilizations**
`/civ create <name>` start a civ (free)
`/civ invite <player>` / `/civ join <name>` / `/civ leave`
`/cc` turn civ chat on or off
`/civ chest` open your civ's shared chest (civ needs 3 members, not within 5 minutes of a fight; more rows are bought in /civ upgrades)
`/civ top` top civs by total honor
`/civ upgrades` buy civ upgrades (leaders and officers)
`/civ upkeep` see each upgrade's daily upkeep
`/civ upkeep pay` pay it, one upgrade at a time (leaders and officers)
`/civ upkeep pause <upgrade>` stop one upgrade's upkeep (leaders and officers)
`/civ guard <player>` make a member a guard (leaders and officers)
`/civ promote <player>` make a member an officer (leader)
`/civ kick <player>` / `/civ disband`
`/civ info` / `/civ list`

**Judges**
Every menu page you open from another page has a Back button in the bottom left.
`/honor cases` open the case menu (confirm, cancel, set amount, rename, fine, case book)
`/honor case <id>` review and rule on a case
`/honor case <id> rename <name>` give a case a name
`/fine <player>` fine a player (chest opens)
`/fine list` / `/fine cancel <id>` open fines
`/honor give <player> <amount> <reason>` award or take honor
`/honor log <player>` honor history
`/honor treasury` open the treasury (paid fines)
`/honor siege <civ>` start a siege on a civ now
`/honor siege stop <civ>` stop a running siege

**Admins**
`/honor reset all` wipe all honor, civs, bounties, cases, fines and the treasury (run twice within 10 seconds, judges stay)
